# Check

**Check** analyzes and elaborates all VHDL files of the project (VHDL-2008).

- Errors and warnings appear in **Problems**, at the line and column of the mistake. Click to jump there.
- Check also runs automatically when you save a VHDL file (setting `nexysvhdl.checkOnSave`).
- Check is quick (about a second) and does not need the board.
