# Edit VHDL

- Put design files in `src/` and testbenches in `sim/` (`.vhd` or `.vhdl`).
- **Project Files** lists your files by role; the top entity is marked `TOP`.
- **Design Hierarchy** shows which entity instantiates which, and updates every time you save.
- Right-click an entity → **Set as Top Entity** to choose what gets built.
- **Open Design Schematic** draws the blocks and port-map connections of one level.
