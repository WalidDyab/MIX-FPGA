# Open the RTL Schematic

**Open RTL Schematic** shows the hardware your VHDL *infers*: registers, multiplexers, adders, comparators and logic gates, connected as synthesis understands them.

It needs a passed Check. Click a block to see what it is and where it comes from in your VHDL; use ⤵ to look inside an instance.

(The Design Schematic shows how your entities are connected; the RTL Schematic shows the logic inside them.)
